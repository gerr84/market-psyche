## A. Gold/silver crash: hear-lag vs react-lag (VERIFIED)
- **Gold 2013-04-15**: -9.3% in a day (worst 2-day drop in 30 yrs, -10% Fri+Mon);
  -25% in 3 months, -30% across 2013. Silver -16.85% Aug-2020 (COVID panic);
  Mar-2020 silver crashed to $11.77 then +148% by Aug.
- **Lag structure**: commodity crashes are GRINDS, not discrete catalysts.
  - Headline within hours of first leg.
  - Retail REACTS split + slow: panic-sellers in first 1-2 sessions; physical
    coin buyers (the "tourist" flush) pile in over following WEEKS.
  - Hear->react: hours (headline) to ~2 weeks (buy-the-dip accumulation).
- Contrast: DeepSeek-R1 (Jan 25-27 2025) was a DISCRETE catalyst -> NVDA -17%
  in ~1-2 days. Sharp narrative shock reprices fast; macro grind reprices slow.
